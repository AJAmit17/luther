# Contributors and acknowledgments

## Creator and maintainer

**[Amit Acharya — @AJAmit17](https://github.com/AJAmit17)** created Luther and maintains the project.

Luther lets people steer coding agents on their own machines from their phones without moving repositories or provider logins into a hosted control service.

## Contributions

Code, tests, documentation, accessibility, design, protocol investigations, and useful bug reports all count. See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).

Git history records individual authorship. `git shortlog -sne` lists authors in the checked-out history. Do not replace existing authorship or upstream attribution with blanket project credit.

## Relay identity

The Relay mark represents the handoff between the phone, the user's machine, and its agents. Geometry and reproducible generation are in `scripts/generate-brand.swift`.

The design is an independent project mark. No trademark registration, originality clearance, or exclusive right to a color is claimed here.
